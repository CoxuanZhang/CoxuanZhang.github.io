# Race to the Sea

*Writen on January 26, 2026\
Inspired by Dan Chiasson's poetry class: to write with you and I*

Big squirrel debates never explained your obsessive aggression\
Jeju years never rid you of the seasickness\
Have you found your way home?\
battling squeeky growls of towering sheep gates,\
tracing mush we made trumping wild garlics,\
sneaking past lads chucking beers in sunken trenches

If I knock at your door,\
will you be old and grown,\
old enough to catch up with\
my speedy nostalgia. *so we race*\
over stinging nettles\
through cambodian harvest\
down rocky cliffs

Then will you dip your tail in the waves\
so I will join you in the salty atlantic,\
saltier than I’d ever let your doggy bowl taste.

– To Fenris, Ahlya’s Jeju Stray Boy
