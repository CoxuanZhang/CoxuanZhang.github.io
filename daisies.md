# Daisies in London

*Writen on February 2, 2026\
Inspired by Dan Chiasson's poetry class: to a friend, against discouragement\
A reference to Věra Chytilová’s film Daisies (1966) that features two girls and their bizarre pranks*

Let's go window shopping!\
when the night captures every waterfront highrise,\
we can pick a life together\
and try not to hold up the self-checkout line with our squabbles.

We will spend the whole afternoon on my carpet over kensington\
stealing street wisedom to play camouflage\
as those who march around barricades of bourgeois white,\
sneer at every motorcyclist tantrum,\
can we always take the first left but never circle back?

We live in a spin-off utopia maybe it’s just\
a merry-go-round that made two-year-old you cry,\
eyes wide open and dazed by vertigo\
growing and growing and\
don’t stop —— the city drowns us in endless dreams
