# Daisies in London

*Writen on February 2, 2026\
Inspired by Dan Chiasson's poetry class: to a friend, against discouragement\
A reference to Věra Chytilová’s film Daisies (1966) that features two girls and their bizarre pranks*

let’s go window shopping\
when the night captures every waterfront highrise\
we can pick a life together\
and try not to haul the self-checkout with squabbles

we study the street wisdom on my carpet over kensington\
play camouflage as those who sneer at every motorcyclist tantrum\
and march around barricades of borgeoise white\
the rule is to always take the first left but never circle back

we live in a spin-off utopia or maybe it’s just a\
merry-go-round that made two-year-old you cry\
eyes wide open and dazed by the growing vertigo\
don’t stop – the city drowns us in its endless dreams
